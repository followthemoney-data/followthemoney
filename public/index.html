What is money supply?

Central banks track how much money exists in an economy using three measures: M1 (cash and instant-access deposits), M2 (M1 plus short-term savings), and M3 (the broadest measure, including money market funds).

When these numbers grow faster than the economy, it can signal inflation. This dashboard pulls the latest figures directly from official central bank sources.
